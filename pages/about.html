About ShuSei Gate

What started from the smallest to the biggest? What attracted from the least to the most? It is us Shusei Gate.

A few years passed and the popularity of the stadium began to rise and rise until it became the most popular stadium for art exhibitions and general entertainment events in the whole of Japan by the time of 1975. The success has brought a bright idea to our founder, Hoshimachi Kote, to start expanding coverage throughout the nation. By the time of 1988, our venue had increased from a mere stadium in Kyoto, to 21 stadiums from the most northern tip of Japan all the way to the southernmost tip.

In 1997, the Internet had become more and more prominent and globalization is the new direction companies are taking. As the type of entertainment people consume changed as well with younger generations preferring more modern entertainment like video games and video form content like YouTube videos, a new wave of entertainment revision ensued. New concepts like Esports Tournament and Anime Conventions emerged and are more popular than ever before. This is when the CEO of the time approached our Malaysian ambassador, to establish the brand in Malaysia. It was a mutually beneficial partnership as it can improve the two nations diplomatic relationship as well as boosting each others nations economy. So the ship of the partnership sailed.

We had our first stadium built in Damansara Heights, in the capital city of Malaysia, Kuala Lumpur. Initially the response from the Malaysian people wasnt up to expectations, but steadily, the popularity grew and even the local businesses and companies started to rent and host their events at our Kuala Lumpur stadium. With the trajectory of our profit and management, there are future plans to further expand our presence in Malaysia. Particularly in the northern side of the peninsula Malaysia, we will be establishing our hub in the Penang state. For the southern peninsula of Malaysia, there is a plan to establish a southern hub in the Johor state, making it accessible to even Singaporeans.
